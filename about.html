I am a wildlife researcher and conservation professional from Assam, India, with a background in Wildlife Science and over three years of field experience in wildlife ecology and conservation.

During my tenure as a Researcher with Aaranyak’s Primate Research & Conservation Division (PRCD), my work focused primarily on the ecology and conservation of the Western Hoolock Gibbon (Hoolock hoolock) across Assam and Arunachal Pradesh. This included population surveys, call-count and acoustic monitoring, habitat assessment, GIS-based spatial analysis and conservation planning. I have also worked on Golden Langur population assessment and human–macaque interactions in Guwahati.

My academic and research interests extend across biodiversity conservation, landscape ecology, habitat fragmentation, GIS and spatial analysis, human–wildlife interactions and community-based conservation. My master’s dissertation examined butterfly diversity and abundance in Bhairabkunda Reserve Forest, Assam, providing additional experience in insect biodiversity and field-based ecological research.

Alongside research, wildlife photography, conservation communication, writing and music remain important parts of my creative practice. I am also the author of Optimus Rhymes: A Flat-Footed Journey.

The common thread across my work is a simple interest in understanding wildlife and its landscapes and communicating that knowledge in ways that can contribute to conservation.
