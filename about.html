I am a wildlife researcher and conservation professional from Assam, India. My work has grown through field research, conservation education, photography, music and writing.

With a background in wildlife science, I am interested in the ways careful observation can lead to stronger conservation and more meaningful communication. I work across forest trails, protected areas, classrooms and community spaces.

Alongside research, I am a wildlife photographer, guitarist and published poet. I am also the author of Optimus Rhymes: A Flat-Footed Journey.
