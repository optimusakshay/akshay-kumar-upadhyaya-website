Fieldwork & conservation

Research is also
about being present.

My work moves between forest trails, protected areas, classrooms and community spaces.

From surveys and habitat assessment to conservation education and capacity building, my experience is grounded in being present with the landscape and the people who share it.

I have contributed to primate research and conservation initiatives with Aaranyak’s Primate Research & Conservation Division, including work connected with the Western Hoolock Gibbon, Golden Langur and Rhesus Macaque.

Conservation education

I have supported programmes that connect students, teachers and frontline forest personnel with the importance of primate conservation. I am interested in conservation that builds understanding, confidence and practical capacity.

Places that have shaped my field experience

My work has taken me through protected areas and human-dominated landscapes across Assam, Arunachal Pradesh and the wider Northeast Indian region.
