About

A field-oriented researcher with a camera, a guitar and a notebook.

I am a wildlife researcher and conservation professional from Assam, India. With a background in wildlife science, my work has grown through field research, conservation education, photography, music and writing.

My interests are rooted in careful observation: understanding wildlife, listening to landscapes and finding better ways to connect people with the living world.
